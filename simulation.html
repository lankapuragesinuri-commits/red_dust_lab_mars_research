DATA INTEGRITY NOTE

The slider advances through the four recorded observation points in sequence; equal slider steps do not represent equal elapsed-time intervals. Values are not interpolated. The second observation's exact time was not confirmed, so it is shown as an intermediate reading without assigning a time. “No measurable output” is displayed as 0 V for the visual replay based on the latest clarification; this is a reported status, not a precision 0.000 V measurement.

HOW TO USE THIS LAB

Run the observation replay

  1. Use the slider to select one of the four recorded observations.
  2. Choose a time-jump button to go directly to a timestamped reading.
  3. Press Play replay to advance through the sequence automatically.
  4. Compare the three unit readouts at each stop.

The replay is a visualisation of your data, not a physical model of a solar panel or a prediction of voltage at unmeasured times.
